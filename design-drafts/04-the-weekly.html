Week 06, Coquitlam. Services rough-in with the ceiling bay open.

4 days behind

Coquitlam kitchen runs four days late after knob-and-tube found above the ceiling

By the project managerWeek 06 of 11$48,200 of $71,000 spent

The panel upgrade on the 1972 rancher opened a live knob-and-tube run in the ceiling bay over the kitchen. It was not visible at the site visit and it was not in the price. We stopped, photographed it, and called the owners the same afternoon.

Change order 02 — $2,100 to remove the run and rewire the bay — was signed on Tuesday. The electrical inspection moves from Thursday to the following Monday, which is where the four days come from.

“We would rather publish a delay than explain one later.”

Insulation starts week 07. Drywall is booked for the week after, and the cabinet delivery date has not moved. We will report against the new date next Friday.
